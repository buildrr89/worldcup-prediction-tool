# Code of Conduct

This project aims to be a welcoming, low-drama place to collaborate on a small
research tool. A few simple expectations:

- **Be respectful.** Treat other contributors and the maintainer with courtesy.
- **No harassment.** Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behaviour of any
  kind is not tolerated.
- **Disagree on the technical merits.** Technical disagreement is healthy and
  welcome; personal attacks are not.
- **Keep issues focused and evidence-based.** Stick to what can be reproduced,
  observed, or reasoned about. Avoid noise, off-topic threads, and speculation
  presented as fact.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may moderate, edit, or remove comments, issues, commits, and
contributions that are off-topic, abusive, or made in bad faith, and may block
repeat offenders. The goal is simply to keep the project productive and
respectful.
